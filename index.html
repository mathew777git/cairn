Meditation

Ends with a gentle bell — no clock-watching. Logs itself when done.

What it doesTrains attention and calm. Practised daily, it's linked to lower stress reactivity, better focus, improved emotional control, and easier sleep — effects measurable within about 8 weeks of regular practice.

HowSit comfortably, spine tall. Rest attention on the breath. When the mind wanders — and it will — notice, and return to the breath without judgment. The return is the rep. Wandering isn't failure; it's the workout.

Quick factTen minutes counts. Consistency beats duration — a daily 10 outworks an occasional 40.
